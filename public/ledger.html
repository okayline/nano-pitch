Every day's deal and every finished game, on one chain: each entry's hash is SHA-256 of the one before it, a bar, and its record, so changing any entry breaks every hash after it. A day's entry fixes the deal and the table's memory before anyone plays; its seed is shown once the day is over, so anyone can rebuild the deals. Today's games stay hidden until then, because everyone holds the same cards. After every seal, the chain's newest hash goes to the shared 88x31 registry as a checkpoint.
