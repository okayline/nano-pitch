Every finished game, on one chain: each entry's hash is SHA-256 of the one before it, a bar, and its record, so changing any entry breaks every hash after it. A result shows the game's number, who finished it, the totals and a hash of their moves, never the cards: everyone who plays game N holds the same deal. Entries from before numbered games (a day's deal, a game with its moves) stay on the chain; those from today are hidden until the day is over, and a past day's entry shows its seed. After every seal, the chain's newest hash goes to the shared 88x31 registry as a checkpoint.
